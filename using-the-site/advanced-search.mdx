## Getting to Advanced Search

Click **"Advanced Search"** in the main navigation or go directly to [catfoodcentral.co/advanced-search](https://catfoodcentral.co/advanced-search)

## Available Filters

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Category & Life Stage" icon="tag">
    **Category** (multi-select)
    Filter by product purpose or formulation type:

    - Hairball

    - Light-weight management

    - Prescription Diets - veterinary formulas

    - Regular - standard maintenance foods

    - Sensitive - for sensitive stomachs or digestion

    - Sterilised - for spayed/neutered cats

    - Urinary - urinary tract health support

    Select multiple categories to see products that fit any of those needs.

    **Life Stage** (multi-select)

    - Adult

    - ALS - All Life Stages

    - Kitten

    - Senior

    → [Learn more about life stages](/faq/life-stages)
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Brand & Flavor" icon="package">
    **Brand** (searchable multi-select)
    Filter by manufacturer with a searchable dropdown showing all 340+ brands:

    - Search bar lets you quickly find specific brands by typing

    - Each brand shows product count in parentheses

    - Select multiple brands to compare products across manufacturers

    **Flavor** (searchable multi-select)
    Filter by primary protein source or flavor profile:

    - Alligator, Beef, Buffalo, Chicken, Duck, Fish

    - Fish varieties: Anchovy, Carp, Cod, Herring, Pollock, Salmon

    - And many more exotic proteins

    - Number in parentheses shows how many products feature that flavor

    Note: Flavor is based on product marketing - always check the actual
    ingredient list to see if that protein is truly the primary ingredient.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Nutritional Sliders" icon="activity">
    **Quality Score** (0-100)
    Set minimum and maximum score thresholds. Recommended starting point:
    60+ for decent foods, 70+ for strong options.

    **Protein %** (20-59%)
    Filter by protein content. Higher is generally better for cats as obligate
    carnivores. The scoring baseline is 50% dry matter protein.

    **Fat %** (7-31%)
    Adjust fat content - useful for weight management or cats with specific
    health needs. Typical range is 10-20%.

    **Fiber %** (0-13%)
    Filter by fiber content - important for hairball control, digestive
    health, or sensitive stomachs. Most foods are 2-6%.

    **Carbs %** (1-51%)
    Filter by carbohydrate content. Lower is better for cats - the scoring
    baseline is 5% dry matter carbs, but most dry foods are 25-40%.

    → [Why are carbs penalized?](/faq/carbohydrates)

    **Metabolizable Energy** (3181-4765)
    Advanced nutritional metric showing available energy content per kg.
    Useful for precise calorie management.

    All sliders show the full range available in the database - adjust both
    ends to narrow your search.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Special Features" icon="shield">
    **Grain-Free** (checkbox)
    Shows only grain-free formulas (412 products). Remember - grain-free
    doesn't automatically mean low-carb or higher quality.

    → [Is grain-free better?](/faq/grain-free)

    **Hypoallergenic** (checkbox)
    Shows foods marketed as hypoallergenic or limited ingredient (119 products).
    Useful for cats with known food sensitivities.

    **AAFCO** (checkbox)
    Shows foods with AAFCO nutritional adequacy statements (250 products).
    This indicates the food meets minimum nutritional standards for stated
    life stages.

    **Bag Size** (multi-select)

    - Small (up to 3.5 kg / 7.7 lb) - 1111 products

    - Medium (4-9 kg / 8.8-20 lb) - 611 products

    - Bulk (10+ kg / 22+ lb) - 404 products

    Filter by package size for storage constraints, trial sizes, or multi-cat
    household needs.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to Use Filters Effectively

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with your must-haves" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Set the non-negotiable filters first:

    - Life stage (Kitten/Adult/Senior)

    - Category if your cat has specific needs (Sensitive, Urinary, etc.)

    - Bag size if you have storage or trial constraints

    - Any allergies or sensitivities (Hypoallergenic checkbox)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add quality thresholds" icon="trending-up" title-type="p">
    Set a minimum quality score - we suggest **60+** as a starting point,
    **70+** for stronger options. Add protein minimum if you're seeking
    high-protein foods (try 40%+ to start).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refine with nutritional targets" icon="sliders" title-type="p">
    Adjust carbs, fat, or fiber based on your cat's health:

    - Weight management: lower fat, moderate protein

    - High energy needs: higher fat and protein

    - Digestive issues: moderate fiber (3-5%)

    - Low-carb focus: set carbs max to 30% or lower
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sort and review" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Choose your preferred sort order (usually Score: High to Low), then browse the results. Click products to see full details, or use the compare feature for side-by-side evaluation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Better Results

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Too many results?" default-open="false">
    - Increase the minimum quality score threshold

    - Add more specific filters (category, protein %, grain-free)

    - Narrow the bag size range

    - Select specific brands you're interested in
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Too few results or no results?" default-open="false">
    - Relax some filters - you might be too restrictive

    - Remove the quality score filter temporarily

    - Check if you've selected conflicting filters (e.g., Kitten + Senior)

    - Try removing nutritional slider constraints one at a time
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Not sure what protein % to target?" default-open="false">
    The scoring baseline is 50% dry matter protein - reflecting a cat's
    natural diet. Most high-quality dry foods land between 40-50% protein
    on a dry matter basis. Start with a minimum of 40% and adjust from there.

    → [Why is protein important?](/faq/protein)
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What's a good carbohydrate target?" default-open="false">
    Cats evolved to consume around 5% carbohydrates. Most dry cat foods
    contain 25-40% due to kibble manufacturing requirements. Aim for under
    30% if possible, under 25% if you're being strict about low-carb.

    → [Why are carbs penalized?](/faq/carbohydrates)
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Should I filter by AAFCO?" default-open="false">
    AAFCO certification indicates the food meets minimum nutritional
    standards for stated life stages. It's a baseline regulatory requirement,
    not a quality indicator. Most foods will have this - it's more useful
    for confirming life stage appropriateness than as a filter.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  The most efficient workflow: filter to a shortlist of 5-10 products, then
  use the compare feature to evaluate them side by side. This is far faster
  than reviewing hundreds individually. Focus on score, protein source quality,
  and carbohydrate levels as your primary decision factors.
</Callout>
