<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Both the main browse page and Advanced Search results support two view
  modes: Grid (default) and List. Your preference is saved automatically.
</Callout>

## Switching View Modes

Look for the view toggle icons in the top-right corner of the results area:

- **Grid icon** (squares) - card-based visual layout

- **List icon** (lines) - compact table format

Click either icon to switch instantly. Your preference persists across sessions.

## When to Use Grid View

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Visual Browsing" icon="grid">
    **Best for:**

    - Browsing without a specific product in mind

    - Recognizing products by package appearance

    - Quickly scanning scores at a glance

    - Getting a feel for what's available

    **What you see:**

    - Large product images

    - Prominent score badges

    - Brand and product name

    - View count

    - Tags (Adult, Regular, Sterilized, etc.)

    Grid view is great when you want to explore visually and aren't sure
    exactly what you're looking for yet.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When to Use List View

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Efficient Comparison" icon="list">
    **Best for:**

    - Comparing many products quickly

    - Seeing more results per screen

    - Scanning key specs rapidly

    - Finding a specific product by name

    **What you see:**

    - Compact rows with product name, brand, and score

    - More products visible without scrolling

    - Quick access to key details

    - Easier to scan alphabetically or by score

    List view is efficient when you know what you want or need to compare
    a large number of products side by side.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Try both! Grid view when exploring, list view when you've narrowed down
  your options and want to compare details efficiently.
</Callout>
