<Callout kind="note" collapsed="false">
  Community reports are one of the fastest ways we catch outdated or incorrect
  product data. If something looks off, tell us.
</Callout>

## What you can report

- Wrong or outdated ingredients

- Incorrect guaranteed analysis (protein, fat, fiber, moisture, etc.)

- A formula that's changed since the product was last reviewed

- A missing bag size

- A product marked available that's actually discontinued

## How to report it

Every product page has a feedback button. Open the product you want to flag and use it to report the issue directly from that page.

Include what looks wrong and, if you have it, a link to the current manufacturer page - that helps us verify and fix it faster.
