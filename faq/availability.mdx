<Callout kind="note" collapsed="false">
  Cat Food Central doesn't currently show prices, sellers, or country
  availability. Right now we focus entirely on what's in the food and how it
  scores.
</Callout>

## Why price isn't part of the score

CFC's scoring methodology is based purely on ingredients and guaranteed analysis - never price. A cheaper food and a more expensive food are scored exactly the same way, based only on what's actually in the bag. This is intentional: it keeps the score an honest reflection of nutritional quality, not a proxy for cost.

## What's not shown today

- Whether a specific product is sold in your country

- Current prices

- Which retailers or sellers carry a product

- Purchase links

## Where to check for now

Check the manufacturer's website or your usual pet food retailer for current pricing and availability. If a product looks discontinued or hard to find, you can [report it](/faq/product-feedback) so we can look into it.
