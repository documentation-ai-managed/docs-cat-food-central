<Callout kind="note" collapsed="false">
  There's no single fixed schedule. Products are monitored continuously, and
  ones being actively watched by users are checked more often than ones that
  aren't.
</Callout>

## How monitoring works

Cat Food Central continuously monitors manufacturer sources for formula and label changes. Products that cat parents are actively watching get checked more frequently - typically every few days. Less-watched products are still checked periodically, typically within about a month.

## What "last reviewed" means

Every product page shows a last-reviewed date. This is the date our process last verified that product's label data - ingredients and guaranteed analysis - against the manufacturer.
