---
title: What is Guaranteed Analysis and how is it used in scoring?
description: >-
  Guaranteed Analysis is the nutritional panel on every cat food bag - listing
  minimum protein and fat, and maximum fibre and moisture. It's the starting
  point for every score calculation.
---
<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Guaranteed Analysis shows minimums and maximums - not exact values. The
  actual protein content could be higher than stated; the actual moisture
  could be lower. Scoring uses these stated values as the basis for calculation.
</Callout>

## What's included in Guaranteed Analysis

Every cat food sold in most markets must declare:

- **Crude Protein** (minimum %)

- **Crude Fat** (minimum %)

- **Crude Fibre** (maximum %)

- **Moisture** (maximum %)

Some manufacturers also voluntarily include ash, taurine, or specific mineral levels.

## How it's used in scoring

<Steps>
  <Step title="Extract the four core values" title-type="p">
    Protein, fat, fibre, and moisture percentages are read from the
    Guaranteed Analysis panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calculate carbohydrates" title-type="p">
    Carbs are not listed directly. They're calculated as:
    **100 - Protein - Fat - Fibre - Moisture - Ash**
    (Ash is estimated at \~6% when not stated.)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Convert everything to dry matter basis" title-type="p">
    All values are converted to dry matter basis to remove moisture
    and enable fair comparison between products.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the scoring rules" title-type="p">
    The dry matter protein and carbohydrate percentages are then used
    in Rules 1 and 2. The ingredient list handles the remaining rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  The Guaranteed Analysis only covers the *amount* of nutrients. The
  *quality* and *source* of those nutrients comes from reading the
  ingredient list - which drives Rules 3 through 9.
</Callout>
