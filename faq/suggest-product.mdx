<Callout kind="note" collapsed="false">
  If a food you're looking for isn't in the database yet, you can suggest it
  directly - we'll review it and add it when we can.
</Callout>

## How to suggest a product

<Card title="Suggest a missing product" icon="plus-circle" href="https://catfoodcentral.co?popup=product_not_listed" cta="Suggest a product">
  Tell us the brand and product name, and a link if you have one - we'll take it from there.
</Card>

Include as much detail as you can - brand, product name, and a link to it if you have one - that helps us find and add it faster.
