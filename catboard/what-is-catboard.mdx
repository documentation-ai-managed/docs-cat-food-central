title: What is Catboard?
description: >-
  Catboard is Cat Food Central's personalization dashboard that helps cat
  parents track their cats and find the best-matched dry food products on Cat
  Food Central.
---
While CFC reviews and scores dry cat food products using an objective, consistent methodology, Catboard lets you apply that information to your own cats, so the guidance you get is tailored to their needs.

## Who Catboard is for

Catboard is built for cat parents who want more than a one-time lookup. If you want to keep track of your cats over time, follow specific products you're interested in, and be notified when something changes, Catboard is for you. It works just as well whether you have one cat or several.

## Is Catboard free?

Yes. Catboard is completely free for everyone to use.

## How to access Catboard

Right now, you can sign in to Catboard using a Gmail or Google Workspace account. If you use a different email provider, you can join the waitlist, and we'll notify you as soon as additional sign-in options become available.
